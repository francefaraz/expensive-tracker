Privacy Policy – Track My Money

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Track My Money (package com.munexa.trackmymoney), published by Munexa Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. Track My Money is an offline personal expense tracker.

1. Data you enter stays on your device

  • Transactions, categories, budgets, templates, bank account and credit card entries, and app settings are stored only in a local database and preferences on your device.
  • The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of ours. We do not collect, upload, sell, or have access to your financial records.
  • For bank accounts and credit cards the app stores only an optional nickname, bank name, balances/limits you type, and at most the last 4 digits of a number. Never enter full card or account numbers, PINs, or passwords.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to Google Drive by the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free versions of the app show ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AD_ID),
  • device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, language, approximate location derived from IP address),
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes related to ads).

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your financial data entered in the app is never shared with advertisers.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required, the app shows a consent form using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings (privacy/consent options). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (such as removing ads or a Pro subscription) are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app only receives a confirmation of which products you own so it can unlock features. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the Google Play Store.

4. Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. No notification content is sent to any server.

5. Export and sharing

When you choose to export, the app creates a CSV file on your device and opens the Android share sheet. The data leaves your device only if you choose a destination (e.g. email, cloud drive); that destination's own privacy policy then applies.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications and run at startup (to restore scheduled reminders after reboot).
  • Internet / network state and advertising ID – used only by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored on your device, you control it. You can delete individual entries in the app, or delete everything by using Settings > Apps > Track My Money > Storage > Clear data or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy that could be deleted on request. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's controls at My Ad Center.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a non-rooted device. Any network communication by the Google SDKs uses encryption in transit (HTTPS).

9. Children

Track My Money is a personal finance tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
